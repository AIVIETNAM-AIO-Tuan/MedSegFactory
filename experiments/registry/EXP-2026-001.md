## Scientific claim and critical review

- Hypothesis/mechanism: spatial pre-op image/mask conditioning of the pretrained
  JCA denoiser improves paired target-informed postoperative reconstruction over
  pre-op copy while preserving stable anatomy and small enhancing labels.
- Null: copy performs as well; gains reflect target-informed text, site/intensity
  translation or extra computation; patient/mask conditions are ignored.
- Cheapest falsifiers: inference-unavailable transform; failed VAE small-lesion
  reconstruction; no improvement over copy; weak sensitivity to a wrong patient
  or wrong mask; stable-anatomy degradation.
- One architecture change: append frozen pre-op image latents and area-pooled
  class fractions to both denoising streams (input 4->11, output remains 4).
  Keep JCA, reference diffusion-noise MSE, timestep policy and CFG ordering.
- Source justification: original text-only model cannot consume patient images;
  this is an adaptation required by the user, not an empirically proven gain.
- No added preservation/segmentation losses, extra UNet, external prior, VLM,
  ControlNet, deformation network or new retrieval database in this experiment.
- Inference inputs: pre-op T1ce, supplied pre-op mask, desired scene text, seed,
  frozen preprocessing. Post-op target/labels/header/registration are unavailable.
- Spatial safeguard: register postoperative training targets into a frame defined
  from pre-op alone. Post-informed input registration is an explicitly separate
  oracle arm and excluded from the primary deployable input contract.
- Text safeguard: TARGET_INFORMED_RECONSTRUCTION uses actual postoperative label
  presence and is explicitly an oracle-text diagnostic. SUPPLIED_SCENARIO uses
  independently specified text and has no unique paired real postoperative GT.

Verified primary sources and limitations:
- Mao et al., MedSegFactory, arXiv:2504.06897v2:
  https://arxiv.org/html/2504.06897v2 — 2D text-conditioned MRI/mask architecture,
  FID/IS and downstream nnU-Net DSC/IoU; no brain benchmark in its dataset table.
- Cepeda et al., RHUH-GBM, arXiv:2305.00005v2:
  https://arxiv.org/html/2305.00005 — collection, preprocessing and supplied labels;
  dataset-reported early timing is not independently verified local hours.
- Brooks et al., InstructPix2Pix, arXiv:2211.09800v2:
  https://arxiv.org/html/2211.09800v2 — input-latent concatenation/zero-initialized
  added channels; natural-image editing precedent, not surgical MRI validation.

Threats: one-to-many outcome ambiguity, future-derived text, registration
selection, tiny lesions, mask-codec artifacts, expert-mask availability, scanner
effects, small sample size and synthetic memorization. Report each explicitly.

## Frozen evaluation before final test

Primary technical endpoint, TARGET_INFORMED_RECONSTRUCTION only:
- R_p = pre-op foreground intersected with a physical 10 mm dilation of all
  supplied pre-op foreground labels. This is an evaluation ROI, not surgery GT.
- L_p = voxel-weighted MRI MAE over R_p on normalized [0,1] images in the same
  1 mm pre-op evaluation frame. Average per-generation MAE within patient before
  macro-averaging patients; do not ensemble images or select best seeds.
- Delta_p = MAE_copy,p - MAE_model,p. Primary effect is mean_p Delta_p, positive
  for improvement. Fixed meaningful technical delta: 0.01 normalized MAE units.
  This is an engineering feasibility margin, not clinical importance.
- Use 10,000 paired patient-bootstrap resamples, seed 20261103, two-sided 95%
  percentile CI. Patients are n; slices/components/seeds are not independent n.
  Average training-run effects within each patient for the primary estimate,
  report every independent run separately and disclose limited power.
- Fix primary/stable ROIs from pre-op independently of predictions. Scored ROIs
  require real aligned-target support and finite prediction throughout; fill/
  padding is not truth. Missing coverage is NA with its assigned-case denominator,
  not a silently reduced ROI. No technical PROMOTE if model-caused failures or
  missing primary/stable endpoints remove locked eligible test patients.
- Amendment 2026-11-03 (user decision before any model scoring): restrict R_p
  and S_p to observed registered target support, report the excluded fraction
  per patient, and score NA above 5%. Non-finite model output inside the
  restricted ROI stays NA and counts as a model failure.

Mandatory technical/safety guardrails:
- Stable ROI = pre-op foreground outside a physical 30 mm dilation of pre-op
  labels. Upper 95% CI for added MAE versus copy must be <=0.005.
- Representation gate on training references, mean AND sampled VAE posterior
  paths: ID-3 positive-patient macro Dice >=0.90; 3D lesion recall >=0.95;
  mean spurious ID-3 volume on negative references <=0.01 mL/patient;
  mean source-unsupported ID-1 volume <=0.01 mL/patient. Record all misses.
- Apply those thresholds to the posterior-mean path and separately to EACH
  sampled path with seeds 11/22/33. Every path must pass; do not average masks or
  select a favorable seed. Dice and lesion recall are patient-macro means over
  positive training references (recall first computed per patient); additionally
  report pooled matched/reference 3D lesion counts. Average phantom ID-3 volume
  over negative training patients and phantom ID-1 volume over all training
  patients, separately for each path.
- Initial resolution 256 at 1 mm/pixel with padding. First fallback 512 with
  unchanged physical FOV. A 192 mm crop is a separate combined adaptation.
  Alternative codecs need an explicit variant/config and repeated gate.

Secondary definitions:
- ID-3 positive-reference Dice/IoU: per patient, empty prediction scores 0.
  Empty/empty cases do not inflate positive-case means.
- Lesions: 26-connected 3D components after full-volume reassembly, no size
  removal. Discard edges with IoU <0.10 before assignment; maximize one-to-one
  match cardinality, then total IoU as tie-break. Use the same rule for the
  representation gate. Unmatched predictions are false positives.
- Empty references: report FP lesion count, FP volume mL and negative-case n.
- Tiny reporting bin: lesions <=0.1 mL versus larger; an engineering bin only.
- Absolute volume error mL; HD95 mm only if both surfaces exist, else NA plus
  missing-surface counts. Report empty reference/prediction conventions.
- MRI MAE/SSIM in the ROI/stable regions, fixed intensity/geometry policy as
  specified in the plan: 1 mm pre-op evaluation grid; axial SSIM 7x7 uniform
  windows, data_range=1, sample covariance, no channel axis; use full-map centers
  whose windows fit valid support and voxel-weight within patient. Empty support
  gives NA. ID-2 adherence is distinct from T1ce image evidence.
- Actual-generated image/mask correspondence needs blinded review or an
  independently validated real-trained reader; paired GT Dice alone cannot
  establish that generated pixels depict their generated mask.
- Cost and denominators: attempted/finite/geometry-valid outputs, error cases,
  runtime, paired samples/s, peak VRAM and checkpoint resume.
- FID/IS: optional exploratory MSF-comparability measures, not acceptance gates.
  Downstream real-test DSC/IoU and utility/noninferiority are later studies.
- Representation posterior seeds: 11/22/33. HD95 boundary/distance convention,
  top-5 nearest-training diagnostics and negative-control frames follow the
  finalized plan and must be fixed in tested metric code before test access.

Clinical review and success definitions:
- Reviewer, rubric and expert review access: not established. Report clinician
  acceptance NOT EVALUATED, never infer it from technical success.
- Technical completion = valid outputs / all attempted outputs; scenario
  adherence = rule-passing outputs / technically valid outputs, with exact rules
  and uncertainty; representation survival = matched / reference lesions.
- Pre/post filtering counts and every denominator must be retained. No selected
  montage or target-informed reconstruction establishes clinical utility.

Decision rules:
- REJECT for leakage, invalid geometry/scoring or demonstrated shortcut.
- ITERATE for a specific repair or a merely promising development pilot.
- INCONCLUSIVE if precision/CI overlaps meaningful benefit/safety boundaries.
- PROMOTE to a larger TECHNICAL study only if primary lower CI >0.01, stable
  upper CI <=0.005, representation gates pass, independent runs agree in direction
  and no unresolved major failure is hidden. No clinical PROMOTE or substitution
  claim follows. Expert-reviewed clinical endpoints/margins require a later card.
- No final-test analysis until metric code, thresholds, seeds, run budget,
  checkpoints and inclusion policy are frozen. Any recipe change after testing
  consumes that test set and requires genuinely independent evaluation for claims.
