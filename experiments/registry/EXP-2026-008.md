## Scientific claim and critical review

Claims, each with its falsifier:

- **C (scaffold).** A deterministic edit that uses pre-op inputs only is closer to the real post-op T1ce than a copy,
  in the frozen primary region. The edit fills the pre-op tumor core with a dark value.
  - Exploratory evidence, on the same 24 accepted RHUH training patients: mean paired MAE gain 0.027 (95% patient
    bootstrap interval 0.019 to 0.035); the scaffold is lower in 23 of 24 patients, and in 17 of 17 without
    residual tumor. Copy median 0.158, scaffold median 0.134.
  - Falsifier: the implemented code does not reproduce the gain.
- **D (localized sampling).** On the frozen r2 weights, sampling that starts from the scaffold and is pinned to the
  source outside the edit region is read at four levels, kept apart:
  - (a) Wiring: the outputs are finite and on the frame's geometry; outside the edit regions the latents follow the
    noised source; after composition every voxel outside `E` equals the source. The last point holds by
    construction of the composition, so it shows correct wiring, not learned preservation.
  - (b) Guardrail: no enhancing label inside the removed core when the scenario says no residual tumor.
  - (c) Mask response (plan item 9): with the mask held fixed to a residual scenario, the image is brighter at the
    scenario's pieces than in a matched run whose fixed mask is empty, everything else being equal.
  - (d) Added value of the model over the scaffold, and of anchoring over editing the whole slice: descriptive only.
  - Falsifiers: (a) a wiring check fails; (b) an enhancing label above the guardrail appears in the removed core in
    2 or more of 3 seeds; (c) the matched difference at the pieces stays within the repeat tolerance in every seed.
    Then no mask response is observed at this checkpoint and configuration.
  - A scaffold left unchanged, with an all-background mask, passes (a) and (b). Passing them therefore means
    readiness for the next experiment, not evidence for H1 or H2.

Null and alternatives: the neural edit adds nothing over the scaffold or makes it worse; localization comes only from
the final composition; the image ignores the fixed mask, or an apparent mask effect comes from the start image; three
seeds and one patient are too few to separate effects from sampling noise.

What stays frozen: the r2 weights, VAE, text encoder, mask codec, 192-mm and 512-px representation, DDIM with 50
base steps, CFG 7.5, the evaluation regions of EXP-2026-001, and RHUH's split. No training in this card.

Inputs at inference, all available before surgery: pre-op T1ce, pre-op mask, and a supplied scenario (residual on or
off, rule parameters, seed). The real post-op scan is used only to score training patients. Those scores are
target-informed and labelled so.

Threats:

- Optimism: the r2 weights were trained on these patients, and the exploratory scaffold result came from them too.
  C4 is a reproduction of the measurement with the new code, not new evidence. The LUMIERE run of C4 is the first
  external check.
- The wiring test uses one patient and a 12-slice slab. It checks wiring; it does not estimate effects, and it says
  nothing about other patients.
- The VAE spreads changes beyond latent cells. Pre-composition drift is reported separately from final identity.
- The composition copies the scaffold outside `E`. A composed image can therefore look clean while the raw decoded
  image drifts or shows boundary artifacts, so raw drift and boundary jumps are measured for every arm and seed.
- A residual scenario's pieces are seeded inside the slab but can grow along the rim outside it. Only the part inside
  the slab is generated and read.
- Real residual tumor is brighter than the same patient's pre-op enhancing tissue (normalized 2.7 against 1.9). The
  rule keeps pre-op intensities and does not correct this.

## Design

**C1 — Scaffold** (`rhuh_synthesis/scaffold.py`, new), in the 1-mm frame:

- Core `K` = pre-op IDs 1 and 3. Fill value `mu` = 10th percentile of the patient's own normalized pre-op brain
  intensity (pre-op only).
- Image: `(1 - w) * pre + w * mu`, where `w` is 1 in `K` and falls linearly to 0 over 2 mm outside `K`.
- Edema and everything else are untouched.
- Correction found at implementation (2026-11-22): this is the 2026-11-17 recipe inside `K` only.
  - In the band outside `K`, the 2026-11-17 script ("dark full core") also mixed in the nearest edema-like tissue.
  - C4 scores both recipes with the same code, so the reproduction and the difference are both measured.
  - Gate C applies to this card's recipe.

**C2 — Residual scenario by rule:**

- Candidate voxels: pre-op ID-3 voxels within 5 mm of the outside of `K`, which is the enhancing rim.
- From a seed: pick 1 to 5 start voxels on the rim and grow the pieces over connected rim voxels, in turn, until
  their total reaches the target volume. Default range of the target: 0.2 to 1.8 ml.
- These bounds are the measured residuals: 0.19 to 1.82 ml, 1 to 5 pieces, about 5 mm thick, 99% within 2 mm of
  the core.
- The pieces keep their pre-op image values (the dark fill skips them; keeping pre-op voxels was closer to the real
  scan than dark fill in 6 of 7 cases). The mask gets ID 3 exactly there.
- The no-residual scenario has no pieces.
- `seed_region` limits where the pieces start, not where they grow: they grow over the whole 3D rim. A run that
  uses a slab records the achieved volume in total and inside the slab, and the components inside the slab.

**C3 — Regions and mask start:**

- Image edit region `E`: `K` plus its 2-mm ramp. Mask edit region `M`: all pre-op labels plus 3 mm (one latent cell).
- Start mask: empty inside `M` plus the scenario's ID 3; the pre-op mask outside `M` (which is empty there).
- This start had the fewest wrong labels against the real post-op masks: 32 ml, against 97 ml for the pre-op mask.
- Prompts: supplied-scenario prompts (`text.scenario_prompts`). On each slice they list edema where the pre-op slice
  has edema, and enhancing tumor where the scenario puts a piece; necrosis never, as in RHUH's post-op labels.
- With a fixed mask (arms W6 and W6e), the mask holds only the scenario's ID 3 and no edema, although the image keeps
  the pre-op edema outside `E` and the prompts may name edema. So these arms test the mask mechanism. They do not give
  a complete post-op multiclass pair, and their outputs are not used as training examples until a later card defines
  how the other classes are composed.

**C4 — CPU check:**

- On the 24 accepted RHUH training patients: primary-region MAE of copy against scaffold (frozen `R_p`, normalized
  [0, 1], post support, 5% cap) with the no-residual scenario.
- Also checked: exact ID-3 pieces, determinism under the seed, and zero change outside `E`.
- Then the same on the accepted LUMIERE training-role pairs of EXP-2026-005 run r2 (25 pairs).
- The cohort is enforced positively: the exports must be exactly the frozen training role minus the acceptance
  policy's exclusions (`scripts/scaffold_check.py`, hardened after the review). Any other patient stops the check.

**D1 — Sampler extension** (`sampling.sample_pairs`, optional argument `edit`; without it the sampler is unchanged):

- Start at `t_start` from the scaffold's posterior-mean latent, noised with a stored base noise. Without `t_start`,
  from the base noise at the first timestep, as the plain sampler does.
- Before each model call, latents outside the latent support of `E` (image) and `M` (mask) are replaced by the
  source latent noised to the current step with the same base noise; after the last step, by the clean source latent.
- The latent support is every 3-mm latent cell that the region touches.
- Fixed-mask option (item 9):
  - The mask rows hold the scenario mask latent noised to timestep 0 with the stored mask noise, as training noises a
    row at timestep 0 (noise coefficient 0.029), and those rows get timestep 0.
  - The scheduler takes one joint step for both streams, and the mask rows are reset after it, so they never move.
    The returned mask is the clean scenario latent.
  - No retraining is needed: training draws every row's timestep separately (`model.training_loss`). This justifies
    testing the fixed mask. It does not show that the image follows it.
- Localized editing runs with `DDIMScheduler` only, the scheduler it is tested with.
- Final composition in voxel space (`scaffold.compose_edit`):
  - Image: `w_E * decoded + (1 - w_E) * scaffold` where `w_E > 0`, and the scaffold elsewhere.
  - `w_E` is the ramp of `K` (`scaffold.edit_weight`). It is 1 on the residual pieces too, so the model's output is
    used there.
  - Mask: decoded inside `M`, the start mask outside. With a fixed mask, the final mask is the scenario mask.
- CPU tests with a small stand-in UNet:
  - empty regions give the source trajectory;
  - full regions give exactly the unmodified sampler with the same noise;
  - fixed mask rows never change;
  - row order and one joint scheduler step per timestep are kept;
  - runs are deterministic.
  - The test that changes the whole mask latent shows that the image depends on the mask rows. That is
    connectivity, not localized enhancement.

## Frozen evaluation (before any run)

- **Gate C:**
  - All unit tests pass.
  - On the 24 RHUH patients, the scaffold's mean paired MAE gain over a copy is positive, with a 95% patient bootstrap
    interval above 0 (10,000 resamples, seed 20261103, as in EXP-2026-001).
  - The 17 no-residual patients are reported separately.
- **Conventions for every D2 readout:**
  - Each readout is named as read on the raw decoded image (before composition) or on the composed image.
  - Volumes are counted in the 1-mm frame after mapping back, so 0.01 ml is 10 voxels. On the model grid a pixel is
    0.375 x 0.375 mm on a 1-mm slice, so 0.01 ml would be 71.1 pixels. Model pixels are never counted as 1-mm voxels.
  - The seed spread is the range: maximum minus minimum over the 3 seeds. Every seed is reported, including any that
    fails a rule.
  - Repeat tolerance: an identical repeat differs by at most 1e-4 in the raw decoded image, and in at most 10 frame
    voxels of the label map. With deterministic algorithms, 0 is expected.
  - The VAE round-trip error is the MAE between the scaffold and the scaffold encoded and decoded without sampling. It
    is the floor for any decoded image, measured in the same region as the readout it is compared with.
- **Gate D (wiring), at the chosen start:**
  - every output is finite and on the frame's geometry;
  - composed image: the largest absolute difference outside `E` is at most 1e-6 on valid support; the same for the
    empty-region check;
  - raw: the full-region check (the localized sampler with whole-slice regions, started from noise) equals W2, the
    plain sampler with the same noise, within the repeat tolerance;
  - the identical repeat is within the repeat tolerance.
- **Guardrail**, in the no-residual localized arms W3, W4 and W5, on the composed mask: less than 0.01 ml of
  enhancing label inside `K`, in at least 2 of 3 seeds. 0.01 ml is the phantom limit of EXP-2026-001's
  representation gate. It is an engineering tolerance, not a clinical safety limit.
- **Mask response (item 9)**, on the raw decoded image:
  - Per seed, `d_pieces` = mean intensity of W6 minus W6e inside the scenario's pieces within the slab. `d_control` is
    the same difference inside control voxels: pre-op enhancing-rim voxels within the slab, outside the pieces and
    more than 3 mm from them.
  - Response: `d_pieces` is positive in 3 of 3 seeds and larger than W6e's seed range inside the pieces. Localized
    response: in addition, `d_pieces` exceeds `d_control` in 3 of 3 seeds.
  - No response: `|d_pieces|` is within the repeat tolerance in every seed. This is an observed negative result for
    this checkpoint and configuration; the idea in general stays open.
  - Anything else: INCONCLUSIVE.
- **Drift and artifacts**, for every arm and seed:
  - Raw drift outside `E`: the MAE between the raw decoded image and the source outside `E`, as a ratio to the VAE
    round-trip error there. Flagged above 2.
  - Edge jump: the mean absolute difference between 6-neighbour voxels whose `w_E` differ, in the composed image, as a
    ratio to the same statistic in the matched scaffold (W1, or W1r for the residual arms). Flagged above 2.
  - A flag is reported with its arm and seed. In a localized arm it blocks PROMOTE.
- **Reported, without a rule:**
  - primary-region MAE of every arm against the real post-op slab (descriptive; one patient; target-informed);
  - W7's enhancing label against its own scenario pieces;
  - W6 against W7 at the pieces;
  - the anchoring contrast W4 against W4g: raw drift outside `E` and edge jump;
  - runtime and peak memory per slice.
- **Start rule:** 501 is used unless the raw change inside `K` stays within the VAE round-trip error inside `K` in all
  3 seeds of W4. The raw change is the MAE between W4's raw decoded image and the scaffold. Then 701 is added as a
  follow-up, with one change. 981 stays only as the contrast.
- **Decisions:**
  - PROMOTE: Gate D, the guardrail and the drift and artifact rules pass at the chosen start. It means the sampler and
    the start are ready for the training series (E runs). It does not establish H1, H2, clinical correctness or use
    on other patients. The mask-response result is reported separately, as a response, no response or INCONCLUSIVE.
  - ITERATE: localized, but enhancing labels appear in the removed core, or a localized arm is flagged. Then one
    change to the mask start or the prompts is tried.
  - REJECT: a Gate D check fails.
  - BLOCKED: Gate C fails. The scaffold is then not a valid start.
