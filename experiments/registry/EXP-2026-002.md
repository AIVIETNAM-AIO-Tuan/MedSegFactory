## Scientific claim and critical review

- Hypothesis: with patient inputs, initialization and random noise fixed,
  scenario text changes the requested synthetic attributes while stable anatomy
  remains preserved. Rewriting the same facts should preserve the scenario.
- Null: initialization dominates, wording changes only incidental pixels,
  or a VLM changes/omits the requested scenario instead of preserving it.
- Measured motivation: Stage 4 r4 improved empty-slice behavior and MRI fidelity,
  but positive-slice ID3 Dice fell from about 0.608 with image-only initialization
  to 0.517 with pair initialization; one positive slice remained missed.
  These are training-set diagnostics, not a supplied-scenario benchmark.
- One proposed change: replace oracle class-presence prompts with independently
  supplied scenario text and controlled rewrites. Keep UNet, VAE, CLIP, image/mask
  codec, geometry, preoperative-pair initialization, DDIM steps and guidance fixed.
- Inputs: the generator receives preoperative T1ce, supplied preoperative labels,
  scenario prompt and seed. Each VLM receives only the same standalone
  preoperative T1ce view plus the same user scenario and declared metadata.
  No postoperative target, target mask, target-derived caption, surgical result
  or future-derived geometry may enter the prompt path.
- Existing comparison grids contain postoperative targets: do not use them as
  VLM inputs. Export standalone preoperative views instead.
- A VLM is asked to express a requested hypothetical outcome, not predict the
  actual operation. Keep observed preoperative facts separate from requested
  postoperative attributes and unknowns. Preserve uncertainty.
- Current generator was trained on short class-presence templates. Rich prose
  tested at inference is outside that training prompt distribution. A poor score
  does not establish that caption-based training or medical VLMs are ineffective.
- Model source, vocabulary, extra facts and length can confound a recipe
  comparison. Use identical requested facts and information access, record all
  unsupported additions, and avoid claiming that medical specialization alone
  caused a difference between different model families.

### User scenarios and RANO terminology check

- Preserve the user's three requested outcome levels and common spatial intent;
  do not silently rewrite a gross-total request as supramaximal surgery.
- Primary RANO resect source: Karschnia et al., Neuro-Oncology 25(5), 940-954
  (2023), DOI 10.1093/neuonc/noac193:
  https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10158281/
- Primary population validation and explicit category table: Johnstad et al.,
  Neuro-Oncology Advances (2026), DOI 10.1093/noajnl/vdag170, Table 1:
  https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC13395087/
- The classification concerns postoperative 3D residual volumes. Class 3 is
  submaximal CE resection (>1 mL residual enhancing tumor): 3A is >1 to 5 mL,
  and 3B, strictly partial resection, is >5 mL. Class 2B is near-total CE
  resection with a small nonzero enhancing remnant up to 1 mL. Complete CE
  resection alone does not establish class 1: class 1 additionally requires
  no more than 5 mL residual nonenhancing tumor; complete CE resection with
  more than 5 mL nonenhancing residual is class 2A.
- Confirmed T1ce screen labels: substantial enhancing residual, minimal enhancing
  residual, and no enhancing residual. These are requested counterfactual attributes,
  not measured RANO classifications of generated samples.
- Sparse 2D slices cannot establish a whole-volume mL threshold. T1ce alone
  cannot establish the nonenhancing component for class 1. RHUH ID2 combines
  peritumoral signal alteration and must not be equated to pure nonenhancing
  tumor volume. No measured RANO-class success will be reported by this screen.
- RANO resect class numbers are unrelated to RHUH label IDs 1/2/3. Spell out
  the intended residual-enhancement attribute; never map a RANO number to a
  segmentation ID or rely on that number alone as the diffusion instruction.
- Common instruction: keep the surgical appearance at the original tumor region,
  maintain patient orientation and distant anatomy, and allow only the slight
  local displacement requested by the user. No numeric brain-shift limit or
  cavity shape is invented. Exact displacement scoring is pending an agreed
  engineering bound; this experiment adds no deformation model.
- A whole-case residual scenario does not require residual tissue or a cavity
  on every axial slice. Slice applicability must use preoperative geometry or
  remain uncertain, never use the actual postoperative mask. Current RHUH
  labels contain no dedicated cavity class; cavity-image adherence requires
  independent review rather than a fabricated segmentation ground truth.

Operational scenario records (same record supplied to both VLMs):

| ID | Requested postoperative condition | Enhancing residual | Common spatial instruction |
|---|---|---|---|
| PARTIAL_CE | Partial/submaximal resection appearance | Substantial, localized to the original tumor bed | Operative bed remains at the original tumor site; only slight local displacement; preserve distant anatomy |
| NEAR_TOTAL_CE | Near-total enhancing-tumor resection appearance | Minimal, localized to the original tumor bed | Same spatial instruction |
| COMPLETE_CE | Complete enhancing-tumor resection appearance | Absent; no claim of supramaximal nonenhancing resection | Same spatial instruction |

- Keep RANO-resect intent as source metadata only (3-family, 2B-like, and
  unassigned, respectively). Do not fabricate a residual mL value or RANO
  classification. The user-approved third scenario overrides the earlier
  provisional class-1 association.
- These are case-level scenarios. For slices outside the preoperative tumor
  region, retain unaffected anatomy and do not require a locally visible cavity
  or remnant. Any per-slice applicability flag is preoperative-only.
- In all VLM requests, explicitly separate desired postoperative appearance from
  observed preoperative findings. Do not present a hypothetical remnant or
  cavity as something already seen on the source image.

- No new mask classes. Preserve IDs 0/1/2/3. A requested cavity appearance is
  not a cavity ground-truth label. T1ce alone does not verify all ID2 findings,
  blood products or ischemia; unsupported VLM assertions count as errors.
- Four requested prompt recipes:
  1. Direct short terms expressing the user scenario, using a fixed template.
  2. General VLM: sentence description of that requested scenario.
  3. Medical VLM: short terms expressing that requested scenario.
  4. Medical VLM: sentence description of that requested scenario.
- Include a fifth recipe: general VLM short terms.
  This completes the source-by-style comparison alongside the direct baseline.
- For each VLM, terms and prose must express the same extracted facts. Store
  both original drafts; mismatched facts are a measured caption failure, not
  quietly corrected for only one arm. Any common repair policy must be frozen
  and raw/repaired results reported separately.
- Additional controls: empty text; a deterministic shuffle of genuinely different
  scenario prompts. Identical shuffled prompts are not a valid negative control.
  The three supplied scenarios provide a paired residual-burden ordering test
  with patient inputs and noise unchanged. Do not invent a surgical scenario
  and attribute it to the user.
- VLMs receive the same images, orientation convention, scenario and instructions.
  Do not give the medical model extra modalities, target labels or extra views.
  Use deterministic decoding and record exact model revisions and generation
  settings. Cache captions and CLIP embeddings before diffusion sampling.
- Generator: saved r3 UNet; selected crop192_512_class_rgb representation;
  r4 preoperative-pair start at t981; 50 DDIM steps; CFG 7.5; no optimization.
- First screen: seed 11, all 16 slices, all arms, at most 112 generated pairs
  per supplied scenario with five recipes plus two controls. Reduce this count
  when a shuffle control is inapplicable; do not replace it silently. Verify
  promising effects with seeds 22/33 using all compared arms under a frozen
  follow-up, not the best seed. Runtime has not been measured for these VLMs.
  With three scenarios, the maximum initial screen is 336 generated pairs;
  identical empty-text results may be computed once and reused explicitly.

## Evaluation to freeze before execution

- First layer, caption fidelity: required user attributes retained / required
  attributes; contradictory attributes; unsupported additions; unknowns retained;
  valid prompt and token-limit completion / all attempted captions. Record facts
  before any repair. Do not use the captioning VLM as its own sole judge.
- Second layer, generator control: paired changes in requested mask-class area
  (mm2 on this 2D screen), class presence and location where the scenario makes
  them measurable; direct image/mask differences at fixed noise; stable-anatomy
  changes outside user-permitted regions. Define the allowed region and any
  numeric adherence threshold only after the scenario is supplied, before runs.
- For the confirmed scenarios, report within-patient ID3-area ordering
  PARTIAL_CE > NEAR_TOTAL_CE > COMPLETE_CE, including ties and each patient's
  result; also report ID3 area for COMPLETE_CE separately. This is a 2D burden
  diagnostic, not a RANO volume/class measurement or a proof of tumor appearance.
  VLMs must preserve residual state, tumor-site relation, slight-local-only
  displacement and distant-anatomy preservation. Contradictions or unsupported
  additional clinical findings are caption failures even when valid JSON or
  fluent prose is produced.
- A nonzero pixel difference establishes sensitivity, not correct adherence.
  A generated mask alone does not prove the MRI depicts that tissue; independent
  image review is required. Report unreviewed image adherence as NOT EVALUATED.
- No Dice, PSNR, MAE or SSIM against the actual postoperative outcome as an
  accuracy endpoint for a different hypothetical scenario. No unique target
  exists. Preservation measures use preoperative anatomy and a declared
  preoperative/scenario-derived edit region, not future target geometry.
- Report all slices and failures, aggregate within patient, and show patient-level
  paired effects. Four patients and one initial seed are insufficient for a
  convincing clinical effect or model-family superiority claim. Any later CI
  must cluster by patient; no slice-level independent-subject inference.
- No clinical meaningful delta or noninferiority margin is claimed in this
  descriptive screen. Scenario-specific engineering rules and review rubric
  remain PENDING and must be frozen before inference.
- Stop: input provenance violation, missing model access, prompt contradictions,
  silent truncation, invalid geometry, nonfinite outputs or class remapping.
  Keep failed attempts in denominators. Do not select only attractive examples.
- Decisions: ITERATE for a reproducible, correctly directed text effect with
  acceptable declared preservation; REJECT a recipe that consistently changes
  the requested facts or damages anatomy; INCONCLUSIVE when the scenario,
  annotation/reviewer or observed effect is insufficient. This experiment cannot
  produce clinical PROMOTE or overturn earlier Stage 4 verdicts.
