## Scientific claim and critical review

Hypothesis: the present anatomy failure can be localized to clean-condition
dependence, initial-latent dependence, or sampling sensitivity without changing
the weights. The null is that our interventions have no material effect, that
different noise explains apparent changes, or that global averages conceal
localized dependence. A channel-dependent output is not necessarily correct.

Measured parent failure: on twelve previews from three validation patients,
step1000 pair-preop brain MAE0.140221 versus copy0.081821; each patient's mean is
worse than copy. Training ID3 Dice0.2885 on four positive previews; validation
previews contain no ID3, although one accepted full volume has a tiny residual.
All180 saved previews and the log were independently checked in the prior review.
The cheap replay reproduces the parent summaries and finite1000-step log.

Ranked falsifiable explanations, declared before diagnostic inference:

1. Weak clean conditioning: wrong/zero channels barely alter epsilon predictions
   or paired loss while swapping initialization has a larger sampling effect.
2. Conditioning without preservation: channels change predictions, but own inputs
   do not improve patient anatomy or prevent changes in stable preop tissue.
3. Guidance/appearance sensitivity: lowering CFG changes brightness error more
   than structural correspondence; improved brightness alone is insufficient.
4. Rare-lesion dilution: dependence or errors differ in target-ID3 scoring regions
   from global error, with low powered residual-positive coverage.

**One proposed change now:** add diagnostic instrumentation only. Freeze model
weights, codec, representation, optimizer history, split, training recipe and
primary Stage5 evaluation. No optimizer steps, new losses, attention blocks,
caption models or trainable settings are introduced.

Sources and interpretation:
- Mao et al., MedSegFactory: Text-Guided Generation of Medical Image-Mask Pairs,
  arXiv2504.06897v2 (2025), https://arxiv.org/html/2504.06897v2 . Section3.1
  defines text-to-pair generation, not patient-preserving pre-to-post editing.
  Section4.6 notes the2D limitation. No brain-surgery benchmark is reported.
- Vendored tutorial_train.py / model.py: the released implementation uses epsilon
  MSE and independent row timesteps; the paper displays a different L1/shared-t
  formulation. Preserve the released-code baseline, document the discrepancy;
  do not silently substitute the paper formula in this diagnostic.
- Native MSF is a text-only reference. Its patient-level reconstruction scores
  are descriptive controls, not a fair claim that it fails its original task.

Inputs: preoperative MRI/masks and target-derived class text, explicitly an
ORACLE-TEXT diagnostic. Actual postoperative pixels/labels are evaluation/loss
targets only, never concatenated conditions or sampling initialization.

## Frozen evaluation and interpretation

Primary diagnostic endpoint: per-patient mean relative epsilon-prediction change
between own and shuffled-both channels, reported separately by model/split and
globally versus brain/edit/ID3 scoring region. Paired MSE changes establish
whether dependence is useful for the specified target, not merely detectable.

Engineering sensitivity bins, not clinical thresholds:
- relative prediction change <=1e-3: weak measured sensitivity in this probe;
- >1e-3: detectable sensitivity, not automatically adequate conditioning;
- donor/own condition tensors differing by <=1e-8: ineffective intervention,
  flagged and excluded from sensitivity-rate denominators, still counted.
- Original-model invariance/equivalence >1e-5: INVALID_PROBE; stop interpretation.
- A weak global response with a stronger ROI response is not 'input ignored'.
- No binary ignored-channel claim from weight norms or one epsilon-MSE average.
- Sensitivity bins are arbitrary engineering descriptions, not validated causal
  or clinical cutoffs. Teacher-forced target-mean denoising dependence does not
  establish useful dependence during a free-running generation trajectory.

Sampling endpoints are descriptive paired brain/stable MAE against own target,
change outside preop-derived edit ROI and target-class Dice/area where valid;
report own-versus-donor and own-versus-copy. No new clinical meaningful delta,
population p-value, clinical PROMOTE or RANO classification is claimed. With
three validation patients, no convincing population superiority can be inferred.
Patient summaries retain failures and missing ROIs. No slice-independent CI.

Stop on invalid hashes, split contamination, same-patient donors, nonfinite
predictions, bad geometry, unexpected channel counts, nonfinite output or altered
weights. Output run IDs are immutable; failed runs retain tracebacks. No costly
training starts from this plan.

Decisions:
- INVALID_PROBE: repair instrumentation; no architecture conclusion.
- CONDITIONING_DIAGNOSTIC_COMPLETE: report sensitivity and paired losses, then
  choose one falsifiable development candidate. This is not a model-quality PASS.
- FULL_DIAGNOSTIC_COMPLETE_UNREVIEWED: sampling artifacts require review.
- INCONCLUSIVE when coverage/control effects cannot distinguish mechanisms.

## Development roadmap after measured diagnostics

1. If clean-condition sensitivity is weak and useful dependence is absent:
   preregister one conditioning/optimization change (e.g. added-channel learning
   rate), retaining matched total steps and a contemporaneous baseline.
2. If conditions matter but stable anatomy changes: test a minimal localized
   editor with explicit preop-derived edit/preserve regions, compare no-op and
   deterministic edits. Copying outside ROI is a construction, not proof of
   learned preservation; independently score edits and boundary artifacts.
3. If anatomy passes but residual control fails: improve scenario/spatial
   supervision with declared provenance. Class presence alone does not encode
   residual burden or cavity geometry. No VLM/architecture expansion by default.
4. Add2.5D/3D consistency only after measured through-plane failure motivates it.
5. Run the frozen Stage5 Part2 when practical; if deferred, retain its pending
   status. A changed method requires a separate experiment/run. Compare final
   candidates on validation, replicate only with approved evidence, keep test sealed.
