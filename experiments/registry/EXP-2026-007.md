## Scientific claim and critical review

- **Claim (round 1).** r2's recipe with LUMIERE's 25 accepted training-role pairs added as image-only rows gives a
  lower primary-region error than r2 on the 7 pooled held-out patients, beyond the seed comparator (below), and
  makes neither cohort worse beyond it. Same steps, optimizer, seed and starting weights.
  - The claim concerns the whole mixing recipe. E3m adds patients, mask rows without a target and always-empty
    prompts at once, so it does not isolate the effect of adding patients (Codex E01, E05).
  - It does not claim an improvement in each cohort: the rule lets one cohort stay within the comparator.
- Why it might work:
  - r2 may be limited by data rather than steps: its validation loss is flat after step 500. This is a hypothesis;
    it does not show that more steps or another objective could not help.
  - LUMIERE adds 25 patients to RHUH's 24 from another site, and adds both partial resections and post-op cores
    that are not dark. RHUH holds only total or near-total resections by design. A post-op K3 above half the
    brain level is found in 11 of 25 LUMIERE pairs against 7 of 24 RHUH patients (C4 region split). The dark-fill
    scaffold cannot represent these cases; a learned edit could.
- Measured failures that motivate it:
  - D2: on one patient no arm beat the scaffold's primary MAE (0.121; W4 0.125 to 0.131).
  - C4 on LUMIERE: the scaffold beats a copy by less than on RHUH (0.0174 against 0.0271).
- Null and alternative explanations, each with its control:
  - Training noise: retraining r2's recipe with another seed (run E0s) measures it.
  - Dilution: in 1,000 steps each RHUH pair is drawn about half as often, so RHUH validation can get worse while
    LUMIERE improves. Results are read per cohort.
  - Site shortcut: the model may render LUMIERE-like images for RHUH inputs (atlas resampling, DeepBraTumIA's skull
    strip, Siemens against GE). Results are read per cohort.
  - Weaker image–mask coupling: image-only rows pair a real image with a mask partner that carries no information.
    Mask readouts on RHUH validation cover this.
  - Too few held-out patients (3 + 4): most outcomes may be INCONCLUSIVE. That is reported as such, not as "no
    effect".
  - Time since surgery (added 2026-11-24, after the literature check):
    - LUMIERE's same-week scans are at most 6 days after the pre-op scan; the day is not released. RHUH's training
      scans are on day 1 in 20 of 24 patients, all at 1.5 T.
    - Benign rim enhancement rises from 40% of scans at 0–36 h to 77% at 60 h or later (Rykkje 2023), and
      methaemoglobin builds up over days 1–21 (Forsyth 1997).
    - So E3m may teach later-looking post-op appearances, and a per-cohort result cannot separate site from timing.
      C4 follow-up 3 (EXP-2026-008) measures wall enhancement in both datasets before any E run.
    - Sources and exact numbers are in `docs/CITATION_LEDGER.md`.
- Cheapest falsifier: round 1 itself. If E3m stays within the seed difference of E0, mixing adds nothing measurable
  at this budget.
- What stays frozen:
  - the EXP-2026-001 training recipe: AdamW, learning rate 1e-5, weight decay 0.01, 20 slice pairs per step
    (4 x 5), bf16, 1,000 steps, the slice-pool rule, text dropout;
  - the representation, VAE, text encoder and codec;
  - the localized recipe (W4 at start 501, DDIM 50 steps, CFG 7.5);
  - the evaluation regions, and both splits.
- Inputs at inference are unchanged: pre-op T1ce, pre-op mask and a supplied scenario. LUMIERE's post-op scans and
  expert ratings enter training only through training-role pairs, and scoring only through development pairs.

## Design

**Runs, round 1 (proposed):**

| Run | Change from E0 | Training |
|---|---|---|
| E0 | none: r2 as trained | none |
| E0s | train seed 2027 (EXP-2026-001's first replication seed) | 1,000 steps |
| E3m | LUMIERE's 25 training-role pairs added as image-only rows | 1,000 steps, seed 2026 |

- Starting point of E0s and E3m (Codex E02):
  - r2's step-0 weights: the author checkpoint with `conv_in` widened from 4 to 11 inputs by
    `model.expand_conv_in` (zero added columns), and a fresh AdamW state. Neither run continues from E0.
  - The author checkpoint's SHA-256 must equal the one in r2's record; the run stops otherwise. r2 recorded no hash
    of its widened step-0 weights, so each new run records one (`e_series.state_dict_sha256`), and E0s and E3m must
    share it. The widening is deterministic (zero columns).
  - The train seed drives every random draw, as in r2's code.
  - The evaluated model is the step-1,000 checkpoint. No other checkpoint, and no choice of the best seed.

**Round 2**, after round 1, with the same evaluation:

- E1: loss weighted to within 10 mm of the pre-op labels, and timesteps up to the start the recipe uses.
- E2: paired left-right flips.
- E4: labelled pre-op rows. D2 showed the failure E4 targets: the free mask kept 17 to 40% of the pieces, and the
  fixed mask did not brighten them.
- Whether these go against E0 or on top of a kept round-1 change is decided with round 1's result.

**LUMIERE rows in E3m** (every choice untested):

- Image row: the post-op T1ce latent, with the normal loss.
- Mask row (Codex E04, E17):
  - What: a pure-noise row left out of the loss. In `training_loss`, the row's noisy input is replaced by the noise
    already drawn for it, its timestep by 999, and its loss weight is 0. No extra random draw is made, so the
    sequence of draws is unchanged.
  - Why: there is no trusted post-op label, and the UNet couples the streams. In its joint attention the image
    half takes its queries from the mask half (`StableDiffusion/Our_Attention.py`), so a partner row must exist.
  - What it is not: it is the absence of a supervised mask target, not a validated "unknown" token. Its own error
    gives no gradient, but the image loss still updates mask features and shared weights through the joint
    attention (Codex measured both on a tiny UNet). So mask behaviour can change; the W7 readout watches it.
  - Forward-noising a mask to t = 999 would keep a signal amplitude of 0.068, so the replacement by pure noise is
    deliberate.
- Loss reduction (Codex E03):
  - The mean over all 2B rows, as in r2, with the weight-0 rows counted in the denominator.
  - So every active row keeps r2's per-row factor, and a batch without image-only items gives r2's loss bit for
    bit. Over many draws the loss is about 0.5 x image error + 0.245 x RHUH mask error (r2: 0.5 and 0.5).
  - Accumulation over the 4 microbatches stays r2's plain mean. Every batch has image rows, so no batch has
    weight 0 throughout.
  - So E3m's logged loss is about (1 + 24/49) / 2 = 0.745 times a loss of the same row errors under r2's
    composition. The totals of E0s and E3m are not compared; the per-cohort row losses in the log are.
- Prompts: empty for both rows, the token text dropout already uses. RHUH's training prompts list the classes of
  the post-op label, which LUMIERE lacks; the expert rating covers enhancing tumor only.
  - Shortcut risk (Codex E05): RHUH empties the image prompt in 10% of draws and both prompts in 5%; LUMIERE in
    every draw. So about 91% of empty-image draws and 95% of both-empty draws would be LUMIERE (expected values,
    not measured). The noise partner is a second cohort cue. Generation at CFG 7.5 uses the empty-prompt
    prediction at every step, so the cue can reach the output.
  - Response: training logs, per cohort, the share of empty prompts and noise partners and each cohort's loss.
    Results are attributed to the combined recipe. Expert post-op ratings never build an inference prompt. The
    text-control arm stays postponed.
- Slices: uniform over the pair's slices, since LUMIERE has no post-op ID-3 pools. Patients: uniform over the 49,
  as now, so about half the draws are LUMIERE.
- Pre-op masks as exported (DeepBraTumIA, renumbered). The C4 region split found no reason to harmonize them for
  the scaffold.
- Never in training:
  - LUMIERE development pairs;
  - RHUH validation or test patients;
  - the about 55 LUMIERE patients kept for phase 3 (item 12).
- Cache and checkpoint checks, when a cache is built and again on every load (Codex E06):
  - Membership must equal RHUH's accepted training patients plus LUMIERE run r2's accepted training-role pairs, by
    dataset-qualified ID. A count of 49 or a folder named "train" is not enough.
  - The cache and the checkpoint's lineage store both split hashes, LUMIERE's acceptance policy and export hashes,
    and the encoder, representation and text settings. Any mismatch stops the run. LUMIERE runs r1 and r2 differ in
    7 of 28 role assignments, so an r1 cache must be refused. The LUMIERE checks reuse those of
    `lumiere_scaffold_check` (split hash, export folder, sidecars); `training.build_feature_cache` has none today.
  - Each item carries a mask-availability flag. An unavailable mask, or LUMIERE's `post_label_unverified`, never
    becomes an empty mask, a prompt or a slice pool.
  - Held-out caches are separate files that the optimizer and the sampler cannot read.

**Evaluation (E-eval)**: the same for every E run, frozen with this card before any E run.

- Held-out sets:
  - RHUH validation: 3 accepted patients.
  - LUMIERE development: 4 accepted pairs (2 CRET, 2 PRET).
  - Reported per cohort and pooled.
  - The RHUH test set stays sealed.
- Generation:
  - Recipe W4: the no-residual scenario, the empty start mask inside `M`, start 501.
  - Seeds 11, 22 and 33.
  - Every axial slice that touches `E`'s latent support. The composition is D1's: outside `E` the composed image is
    the scaffold, so other slices are not generated.
  - Generated slices are spliced into the full-volume frame, and every other slice is the frozen scaffold. Affine,
    coverage and axial indices are kept, and scoring uses the whole frame, not the generated subset (Codex E07).
  - Conditions come from pre-op inputs and the supplied scenario only: `localized_wiring.SlabSampler`'s scenario
    prompts. The prompts of the training cache and of `pilot.condition_cache` derive from post-op labels and are
    never used for generation (Codex E07).
  - Test: changing or removing a held-out patient's post-op image, label or rating leaves the generation inputs
    and outputs unchanged, with the pre-op inputs, the scenario and the stored noise fixed.
- The scaffold and the copy are scored on the same volumes.
- **Primary endpoint:** primary-region MAE of the composed volume against the real post-op scan, per patient: the
  mean of the 3 per-seed MAEs, never the MAE of a mean image. Scored by `controls.image_scores` (the frozen `R_p`,
  the support restriction and the 5% cap). Lower is better.
  - What it measures (Codex E08): paired reconstruction under a fixed no-residual scenario, not scenario fidelity.
    Two LUMIERE development pairs are PRET and RHUH residual status varies, so a lower error can reward drawing a
    residual the scenario did not ask for.
  - Residual status (RHUH) and CRET or PRET (LUMIERE) are reported as strata, descriptively. No patient leaves the
    7-patient denominator after results are seen.
  - Predictions must be finite wherever the score reads them. A non-finite or missing prediction stops the run; it
    never shrinks the scored region (Codex E13: `image_scores` itself drops non-finite predictions). Crop coverage
    is recorded separately.
- Secondary:
  - primary-region SSIM, with `controls.image_scores`'s settings;
  - raw drift outside `E` as a ratio to the VAE round trip, as in D2's frozen readout; NA when the round-trip
    error is 0;
  - enhancing label generated inside `K`, in ml of 1-mm frame voxels;
  - held-out diffusion loss, a diagnostic (Codex E09):
    - on each patient's generation slices, with posterior-mean latents and stored noise (fixed per patient, slice
      and timestep), at image timesteps 100, 300 and 500. These are fixed probes, not the 26-step schedule from
      501 down to 1;
    - per patient, then per cohort;
    - two conditions, never mixed: image-only (mask partner pure noise at 999, empty prompts; both cohorts), and
      oracle-conditioned (target mask latent and target-derived prompts, as `overfit.fixed_eval_loss` does now;
      RHUH only). The second is labelled oracle and is not an inference measure.
- Mask readout on RHUH validation (Codex E10):
  - What: recipe W7 (a rule residual) on a 12-slice slab. Each patient's scenario seed, slab, support and
    generation noise are fixed and the same for every model. Requested, achieved and in-slab piece volumes are
    recorded.
  - Kept share, per seed: enhancing-label voxels inside the in-slab pieces divided by the in-slab piece voxels,
    both within the support, in 1-mm frame voxels. Per patient: the mean over the 3 seeds. Readout: the mean over
    the 3 patients. A patient without an in-slab piece is NA and stays counted.
  - Also reported: Dice and precision against the pieces, and the enhancing volume off the pieces in ml. Labelling
    the whole core would maximize the share alone.
  - Why: it tests whether image-only rows weaken the mask stream.
- Memorization alert (item 11), on CPU from the saved stacks (Codex E11):
  - Coordinates: the model grid. For each generated slice, the query region is its fixed supported part of `E`.
  - Candidates: training post-op slices at the same position on the model grid with at least 100 voxels of that
    region inside their support. Constant or empty regions are NA.
  - Statistic: the maximum Pearson correlation. A correlation above 0.95 is an alert.
  - Banks: RHUH's 24 training patients as the common bank for comparing models, and each model's full training
    bank (E3m adds LUMIERE's 25) for its own audit. Bank sizes and the copy's and scaffold's alert rates are
    reported; matches are kept privately.
  - An alert is not proof of memorization, and no alert does not show privacy.
- Comparisons: each run against E0, paired by patient; against the scaffold; against the copy.
- Seed comparator from E0s, fixed before either result (Codex E12):
  - MAE: the mean over patients of the absolute per-patient difference |E0s - E0|, pooled (7) and per cohort (3
    and 4), in normalized MAE units.
  - W7: the mean over the 3 RHUH patients of |kept share of E0s - kept share of E0|, in share units.
  - Absolute values, so that differences cannot cancel.
  - Limitation: two trained models neither estimate nor bound the seed variance. Exceeding the comparator is
    development evidence, not proof of reproducibility; staying within it says nothing general about mixing.

## Frozen evaluation (approved with decisions 5 and 8 on 2026-11-25, specified after Codex's review)

- Effect of a run (Codex E13):
  - Per patient: delta = run MAE minus E0 MAE, each the mean of its 3 per-seed MAEs.
  - Pooled: the mean over the 7 patients, with equal patient weights (3/7 RHUH, 4/7 LUMIERE).
  - 95% interval: a patient bootstrap stratified by cohort, resampling 3 and 4 within each (10,000 resamples, seed
    20261103). The unit is the patient: n = 7, never 21 or the slice count.
  - Per-cohort values are descriptive.
- **Keep the change (PROMOTE)** if all of these hold:
  - the pooled delta is below 0 and its interval is too;
  - its size exceeds the pooled seed comparator;
  - neither cohort's mean delta is above that cohort's comparator;
  - the guardrail holds: less than 0.01 ml of enhancing label inside `K` in at least 2 of 3 seeds, for every
    patient;
  - W7's mean kept share is not below E0's by more than the W7 comparator. If W7 is NA for all 3 patients, PROMOTE
    is not possible.
- **REJECT** if the pooled delta is above 0, with its interval above 0 and its size above the pooled comparator,
  or if the guardrail fails.
- **INCONCLUSIVE** otherwise. This is the likely outcome with 7 patients, and it is reported with every number.
- **BLOCKED** if a run fails, or if any held-out patient lacks a primary value in any seed. A missing seed is never
  dropped from an average.
- Precedence: BLOCKED, then REJECT, then PROMOTE, then INCONCLUSIVE. A missing secondary value is reported as NA
  and decides nothing.
- Value over the scaffold (decision 8, from Codex E14): a run adds value only if its pooled gain over the scaffold
  has a 95% lower bound above 0.01, EXP-2026-001's meaningful delta and promotion rule. A gain of at least 0.01
  whose interval only excludes 0 is reported as such, not as value above 0.01. This is reported, and it is not
  needed to keep a change.
- Not claimed: realism, clinical validity, use on other patients.

## Budget

- Training: about 1 hour per run on the G4. r2 took 38 minutes of training, 63 minutes in total, peak 64.9 GiB.
  E3m's feature cache is about twice r2's.
- Evaluation: estimated from D2's throughput (about 3 s per slice and seed at 26 steps, batch 6). That is about 1
  hour per model for 7 patients, roughly 50 slices each, and 3 seeds. It is measured on the first evaluation.
- Round 1: 2 trainings and 3 evaluations, about 5 GPU hours.
- Order, decision 9 (from Codex E16, approved 2026-11-25):
  - CPU contract tests first.
  - Then one Colab session with a real forward and backward pass on labelled-only, image-only and mixed
    microbatches (finite losses, the intended gradient paths), and E0's E-eval.
  - E0's record must hold all 7 primary values, the frozen prompts, scenarios and noise, the full-frame
    composition, and metrics that recompute identically from the saved stacks. It also measures slice counts,
    memory and runtime.
  - The two trainings start only after that. A failed contract stops the series. No GPU time is added.

## Implementation plan (after approval)

1. Data: `training.build_feature_cache` accepts exports without a post-op label as image-only items, with a
   mask-availability flag. Their mask latents are unused, and their prompts are empty. `SliceSampler` draws their
   slices uniformly. The cache and lineage checks above run on build and on load.
2. Loss: `model.training_loss` gets an explicit per-row contract (Codex E17), because today it draws every row's
   timestep and noises every row itself, so passing noise in from `train_step` would be noised again.
   - Per-row loss weights, with the reduction above.
   - For image-only items, the mask row's noisy input is the noise drawn for that row and its timestep is 999.
   - Without image-only items, the random draws and the loss are bit-identical to r2's (test).
3. E-eval module, which reuses `localized_wiring`:
   - full-volume localized generation for one patient;
   - scoring, held-out loss, the W7 slab readout and a write-once record.
4. Notebooks, writing through the Drive API:
   - E-train, from the Stage 5 notebook, with `configs/experiments/EXP-2026-007_<run>.json`;
   - E-eval.
5. Tests, local CPU with a tiny random UNet:
   - r2's loss path unchanged, random draws included;
   - the UNet's actual inputs and timesteps are captured: image-only mask rows get the drawn noise at 999;
   - weight-0 mask rows give no gradient through their own error, while the image loss may reach mask features
     through the joint attention (allowed, not tested as zero);
   - labelled-only, image-only and mixed batches give finite losses;
   - cohort and cache guards: no development, validation, test or item-12 patient; a stale or mismatched cache is
     refused;
   - generation does not change when held-out post-op data change;
   - E-eval on a synthetic patient, with the metrics recomputed from the saved stacks.
   - No training is simulated on the CPU.

## Inputs from the literature check (added 2026-11-24, proposals only)

The checked sources, with their numbers, are in `docs/CITATION_LEDGER.md`. Each element below is a proposal for
round 2 or later, gated by a CPU check first.

- **Cavity content as a scenario element.**
  - Evidence: C4 follow-up 2 (EXP-2026-008) found the scaffold's losses in cores that are not dark on T1, are very
    bright on FLAIR and do not enhance. Blood in or near the cavity is common on early scans (63% intradural at
    12–48 h, median 0.4 mL; Johnstad 2024), and liquid or bloody fluid looks different from clot.
  - Proposal: a supplied "dark" or "not dark" cavity, tested first on CPU with the level matched to each pair's
    real outcome (EXP-2026-008).
  - Update after C4 follow-up 4 (EXP-2026-008, 2026-11-25, both datasets):
    - At each pair's own real level, a flat fill beats the copy in every pair, all 5 losses included. The level is
      the right parameter, as far as the primary MAE can tell. The MAE cannot show whether texture matters.
    - A typical level for the class (dark or not dark on T1) is not shown to help over all pairs in either dataset.
      The classes' levels overlap, so a class read on T1 predicts the T1ce level only roughly.
    - Revised proposal: the cavity content as a level f in the scenario (fill = mu + f x (brain - mu)), drawn from
      the training-role pairs' own levels. Whether a class in the prompt adds anything is not tested.
    - Not tested: whether the localized sampler carries a fill level other than `mu` into its output. D2's item 9
      found that the start dominated the output at the residual pieces, which suggests it would. A GPU check comes
      first.
- **Benign rim enhancement as a scenario element.**
  - Evidence: the most common benign early finding.
  - Proposal: thin (under 3 mm), smooth, along the cavity wall, labelled background (not rET), with a probability
    taken from the literature by the time since surgery.
  - Our data cannot learn the time effect: RHUH is at day 1 in 20 of 24, and LUMIERE has weeks only. Any time input
    would come from literature priors on a rule-based element.
  - Update after C4 follow-up 3 (2026-11-24, both datasets):
    - Voxel-level wall enhancement above background is rare in both datasets (1 of 17 RHUH and 1 of 19 LUMIERE
      complete resections above +0.05), so this element has low priority for these data.
    - A T1-bright wall is more common in LUMIERE: 8 of 19 against 2 of 17, a count not planned before the run. This
      supports the cavity-content element and EXP-2026-009.
- **Pre-contrast T1 generated with T1ce.**
  - Evidence: intrinsic T1 brightness from blood is the most common mimic of residual enhancing tumour, and only
    pre-contrast T1 separates it.
  - Proposal: a separate card, EXP-2026-009 (draft), because it changes what the model generates.
